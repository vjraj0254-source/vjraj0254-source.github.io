About Me

What I Bring

  • ⚙️ Real-world pipeline & field construction experience
  • 🤖 Autonomous robotics: ROS2, SLAM, Navigation2
  • 📋 Code-compliant thinking (ASME B31.3, BPVC)
  • 💻 Software development (Python, Java, Linux)
  • 📐 Engineering design (AutoCAD, MATLAB, Gazebo)
  • 🏆 Six Sigma Green Belt — process optimization mindset
  • 🧩 Systems thinking across mechanical + digital domains

My engineering career started on gas pipeline construction sites — learning through trenching, joint coating, crossings, and mechanical installations with STRIA Constructions. That hands-on foundation shaped how I think about problems: practically, systematically, and with an awareness of what happens when things go wrong in the field.

Looking to grow beyond the field, I pursued a Master of Engineering at Old Dominion University with a focus on robotics. For my capstone, I built an autonomous mobile robot using ROS2, SLAM, LiDAR, and Navigation2 — integrating sensors, mapping algorithms, and path planning into a working system. It was challenging and rewarding in equal measure.

I'm someone who enjoys working across disciplines — whether that's reading pipeline specs, writing Python scripts, or debugging a navigation stack. I hold a Six Sigma Green Belt, have field QC experience under ASME codes, and have done software development work as well. I'm always looking to keep learning and contribute wherever I can add value.
